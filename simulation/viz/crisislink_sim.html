Watching one message cross the mesh

Fifty phones move between shelters with no network. Every strategy below sees the same people, the same movement and the same messages. Pick a message and see which phones end up carrying a copy.

This is one simulated world (seed ), so the numbers are noisy. Across 30 worlds the delivery rates are 0.69 for flooding, 0.81 for Spray-and-Wait and 0.75 for the learned policy; the learned policy sends far fewer handoffs than flooding and reaches people sooner, but Spray-and-Wait still delivers more.
